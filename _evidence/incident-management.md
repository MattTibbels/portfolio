# Incident Management and Troubleshooting

## What this demonstrates

This capability area shows my ability to diagnose issues, restore service, communicate clearly and reduce the chance of recurrence.

## Representative examples

- Core network fault diagnosis and restoration
- Wi-Fi authentication issue triage
- Access point replacement
- Phishing report validation and escalation

## Common pattern

Across these examples, my approach is to:

1. validate the issue
2. assess scope and impact
3. restore service where possible
4. communicate clearly with affected users or stakeholders
5. document the issue
6. identify prevention or process improvement opportunities

## Relevant roles

- Service Desk
- Network Administrator
- Systems Administrator
- ICT Project Officer
- Technical Support Officer
